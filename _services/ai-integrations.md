## AI Models & Ecosystems We Integrate

We select, fine-tune, and integrate the best AI models based on your cost, speed, and privacy requirements:

* **OpenAI (ChatGPT / GPT-4o / O1):** Industry standard for multi-turn conversations, complex reasoning, structured JSON outputs, and function calling.
* **Anthropic Claude (Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Opus):** Exceptional for long-context understanding, agentic coding workflows, and precise document/code analysis.
* **Google Gemini (Gemini 1.5 Pro / Flash):** Unmatched multimodal capabilities (text, images, audio, video) and massive context processing.
* **DeepSeek & Open-Weight Models (Llama 3, Qwen, Mistral):** High-performance, cost-effective, or self-hosted models for complete data privacy and air-gapped security.

## What We Offer

### 1. E-Commerce & Web Application AI Solutions
* **Smart Conversational Assistants:** Embed context-aware chatbots (powered by ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) trained on your product catalog for customer support and order tracking.
* **Semantic Search & Recommendations:** Integrate vector search (via OpenAI or Gemini embeddings) for natural-language product discovery.
* **Automated Content & SEO Generation:** Generate high-converting product descriptions, category metadata, and blog content automatically via LLM pipelines.

### 2. Custom Software & Backend AI Pipelines
* **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration:** Securely connect AI models to your internal databases, Magento backend, CRM, and third-party APIs.
* **Agentic Workflows & Automation:** Deploy autonomous AI agents using Claude or GPT to handle leads processing, invoice extraction, and data triage.
* **Predictive Analytics & Insights:** Harness historical sales data to forecast inventory requirements and automate dynamic pricing rules.

### 3. Mobile & Cross-Platform Integrations
* **App-Native AI Capabilities:** Integrate real-time voice, vision (image processing), and personal assistants into iOS and Android apps.
* **Middleware & API Proxies:** Custom lightweight backend middleware to handle prompt management, model switching, fallback handling, and token cost optimization.
